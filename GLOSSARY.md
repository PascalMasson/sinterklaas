# Sinterklaas

The shared language for participant lists, gifts, reservations, visibility, and foppers.

## Language

**Cadeau**:
A gift entry on a participant's list, including its description, location, status, and visibility.

**Cadeau-ingestion**:
The act of bringing cadeau data from an external representation into the application's canonical cadeau data.

**Legacy snapshot**:
An exported representation of the earlier Sinterklaas data set, containing cadeau and fopper records.

**Visibility**:
The rule that determines which participant can see a cadeau and which participant can configure that rule.

**Reservation**:
A participant's claim on a cadeau in another participant's list, represented by a cadeau status and the participant who claimed it.

**Participant list**:
The collection of cadeaus owned by one participant and addressed by that participant's identity.

**Media preview**:
The display data needed to show an attached image or non-image file to a participant.
